About This Tool

The Word to Excel converter pulls tables out of a Word document and rebuilds them as a proper spreadsheet — useful when someone sends you a report or form as a .docx file and you need the data in Excel to sort, filter, or calculate with it. Plain paragraphs of text are skipped automatically, since this tool focuses on table data only.

How to Convert Word to Excel

1
Upload your file. Drag and drop your .docx file into the box above, or click it to browse your device.
2
Convert. Click "Convert to Excel." Tables in your document are detected and rebuilt as spreadsheet sheets right in your browser — no waiting on a server.
3
Download. Once you see the success message, download your new .xlsx file and open it in Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice Calc.

All conversion happens locally in your browser using JavaScript — your document is never uploaded to a server. Once the Excel file is generated, the source data is held only in your browser's memory and is automatically cleared when you select a new file or leave the page. We never see, store, or have access to your files.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Word to Excel converter free?

Yes. Converting Word documents to Excel is completely free on Numvexa, with no signup, watermark, or file limit.

Does it convert the whole document or just the tables?

This tool extracts only the tables in your Word document. Regular paragraphs and text outside of tables are ignored, since they don't map to spreadsheet rows and columns.

What happens if my document has multiple tables?

Each table found in the document becomes its own sheet in the resulting Excel file, named Table1, Table2, and so on in the order they appear.

Is it safe to convert sensitive documents here?

Yes. The conversion happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your file is never uploaded to a server, so sensitive data never leaves your device.

Does this support .doc files?

Only modern .docx files are supported. If you have an older .doc file, open it in Word and use "Save As" to convert it to .docx first.
