Frequently Asked Questions

What is the current home loan interest rate?

Home loan rates vary by lender and borrower profile. SBI, HDFC, and ICICI typically offer 8.5–9.5% p.a. for floating-rate home loans (subject to change).

What is the maximum home loan tenure?

Most banks offer home loan tenure up to 30 years. Longer tenure = lower EMI but significantly higher total interest.

What is the tax benefit on home loan?

Principal repayment qualifies for 80C deduction (up to ₹1.5L). Interest paid qualifies for 24(b) deduction (up to ₹2L for self-occupied property under old regime).

What is LTV ratio?

LTV (Loan-to-Value) is the loan amount as a percentage of property value. Most banks offer up to 80–90% LTV (i.e., 10–20% down payment required).
