About Numvexa

India's go-to platform for free, accurate, and easy-to-understand financial calculators. Built for real people making real financial decisions.

Our Mission

Financial literacy in India is growing fast, but the tools to support it haven't kept up. Banks bury real numbers in fine print. Loan agents quote rounded EMIs. Tax consultants charge for calculations you can do in seconds with the right tool.

Numvexa was created to change that. Our mission is simple: give every Indian — whether they earn ₹20,000 a month or ₹20 lakh — access to the same quality of financial insight that professionals use. No sign-up required. No paywalls. No unnecessary complexity.

This website is owned and maintained by Raja Mahakul, a developer passionate about making financial tools accessible to everyone.

Numvexa is a comprehensive collection of financial calculators covering the most common money decisions Indians face every day. Every tool gives you instant, step-by-step results you can actually understand.
