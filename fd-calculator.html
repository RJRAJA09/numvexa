Frequently Asked Questions

How does FD work?

An FD is a term deposit where you invest a lump sum for a fixed period at a guaranteed interest rate. Most banks compound interest quarterly.

What is the highest FD interest rate?

FD rates vary by bank and tenure. Small Finance Banks often offer 8–9% p.a., while major banks offer 6.5–7.5% p.a. (subject to change).

Is FD interest taxable?

Yes. FD interest is taxable as 'Income from Other Sources' at your slab rate. Banks deduct TDS at 10% if interest exceeds ₹40,000/year (₹50,000 for seniors).

What is cumulative vs non-cumulative FD?

Cumulative FD reinvests interest (compound effect). Non-cumulative FD pays out interest periodically (monthly, quarterly, or annually).
