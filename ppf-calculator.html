Frequently Asked Questions

What is PPF?

PPF (Public Provident Fund) is a government-backed long-term savings scheme with a 15-year lock-in. It offers EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) tax status.

What is the current PPF interest rate?

The current PPF interest rate is 7.1% per annum, compounded annually. The government reviews rates quarterly.

What is the PPF investment limit?

Minimum: ₹500/year. Maximum: ₹1,50,000/year. Deposits exceeding ₹1.5L per year do not earn interest.

Can I extend PPF after 15 years?

Yes. You can extend in blocks of 5 years with or without further deposits, continuing to earn interest.
