How Splitting a PDF Works

This tool copies the pages you choose into new PDF file(s) without re-rendering them, so quality, text, and links stay exactly as in the original document.

1
Choose your PDF. Click the box above or drag and drop a PDF file.
2
Pick a mode. Extract a specific page range, or split every page into its own file.
3
Split. Click "Split PDF" and your new file(s) are created instantly.
4
Download. Download links appear right away — nothing is sent to a server.

Password-protected PDFs can't be split, since the page contents can't be read in the browser without the password.
