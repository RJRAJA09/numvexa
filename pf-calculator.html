Frequently Asked Questions

What is EPF?

EPF (Employees' Provident Fund) is a retirement savings scheme under EPFO. Both employee and employer contribute 12% of the basic salary monthly.

What is the current EPF interest rate?

The EPF interest rate for 2023-24 is 8.25% per annum, declared by the EPFO annually. Interest is calculated monthly but credited annually.

Can I withdraw EPF before retirement?

Partial withdrawal is allowed for specific purposes (home purchase, illness, marriage). Full withdrawal is allowed on retirement (58 years) or after 2 months of unemployment.

What is VPF?

VPF (Voluntary Provident Fund) allows employees to contribute more than the mandatory 12% to their EPF account at the same interest rate.
