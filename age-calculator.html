Frequently Asked Questions

How is exact age calculated?

We calculate the difference between your date of birth and today's date, counting completed years, then remaining months, then remaining days.

What is the total days calculation?

Total days = (Today – Date of Birth) in milliseconds divided by milliseconds per day.

Can I calculate age on a future date?

You can set 'As of date' to any future date to find your age at that point in time.

Why might my age differ by 1 day?

This can happen due to time zones. Our calculator uses your local system date for calculation.
