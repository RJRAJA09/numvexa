Frequently Asked Questions

What is CTC?

CTC (Cost to Company) is the total annual expense a company incurs on an employee, including basic salary, HRA, allowances, PF contributions, gratuity, and other benefits.

How is in-hand salary calculated?

In-hand = CTC – Employer PF – Gratuity provision – Employee PF – Professional Tax – Income Tax (TDS)

What is the PF deduction?

Employee PF = 12% of Basic Salary (capped at ₹1,800/month if basic exceeds ₹15,000). Employer also contributes 12% (partially to EPS).

What is professional tax?

Professional tax is a state-level tax deducted monthly from salary. It varies by state (max ₹200/month in most states, ₹2,500/year cap).
