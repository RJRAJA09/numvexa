Frequently Asked Questions

What is the moratorium period in education loans?

The moratorium period is the time after course completion (usually course duration + 1 year) during which no repayment is required. Interest may or may not accrue.

What is the education loan interest rate?

Education loan rates typically range from 8% to 13% p.a. Government schemes like Padho Pardesh offer subsidized rates.

Is education loan interest tax-deductible?

Yes. Under Section 80E, the interest paid on education loans is fully deductible from taxable income for up to 8 years starting from the year repayment begins.

What is the maximum education loan amount?

Bank limits vary: ₹10L for studies in India, up to ₹20L or more for abroad. Collateral is typically required for loans above ₹7.5L.
