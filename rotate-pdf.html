How Rotating a PDF Works

This tool updates the page-rotation setting stored inside the PDF itself — the underlying page content isn't re-rendered, so text and image quality stay exactly as in the original file.

1
Choose your PDF. Click the box above or drag and drop a PDF file.
2
Pick a rotation angle. Choose 90°, 180°, or 270° clockwise — applied to every page.
3
Rotate. Click "Rotate PDF" and the new orientation is applied instantly.
4
Download. Your rotated PDF downloads straight to your device.

This rotates every page by the same amount. If you only need to fix one or two pages within a longer document, use Split PDF first to separate them, rotate that smaller file, then Merge PDF to put everything back together.
