Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: June 1, 2026  |  Owner: Raja Mahakul

Welcome to Numvexa (https://numvexa.in/). Your privacy matters to us, and we've written this policy to be as clear and readable as possible. This document explains what information we collect when you visit Numvexa, how we use it, and what choices you have. By using this website, you agree to the practices described here.

💡 Short version: We don't collect your calculator inputs. We use Google Analytics for traffic insights and Google AdSense for advertising. We never sell your data. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide Voluntarily

If you reach out through our contact form or email us directly, we may receive your name and email address. This information is used only to respond to your message and is not stored in any database or used for marketing.

b) Automatically Collected Technical Data

Like most websites, Numvexa may receive standard technical information when you visit — things like your browser type, operating system, the page you came from, and which pages you view. This data is collected in aggregate through analytics tools and is not linked to your identity.

c) Calculator Inputs

All calculator inputs — including loan amounts, salary figures, investment amounts, and tax details — are processed entirely within your browser using JavaScript. This data is never transmitted to our servers. We have no record of what you calculate.

2. Cookies Policy

Numvexa uses cookies — small text files placed on your device — primarily through the third-party services described below. We do not use cookies to track you across the web independently.

  • Analytics cookies – Used by Google Analytics to understand visitor behaviour in aggregate (pages visited, time on site, device type). These are anonymised and cannot identify you personally.
  • Advertising cookies – Used by Google AdSense to display relevant ads. These may track browsing behaviour across websites to personalise the ads you see.
  • Functional cookies – We store your dark/light mode preference locally in your browser using localStorage. This is not transmitted anywhere.

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling advertising cookies does not remove ads — it just makes them less personalised.

3. Analytics Usage

We use Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) to understand how visitors interact with Numvexa. This helps us identify which calculators are most useful, which articles resonate with readers, and where we can improve the site. The data collected is anonymised and aggregated — we cannot use it to identify individual visitors.

Google Analytics may set cookies on your device. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Google AdSense Usage

Numvexa displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other sites across the internet. This allows Google and its advertising partners to show you ads that are relevant to your interests.

Google's personalised advertising is governed by Google's own privacy policy and advertising policies. You have the following opt-out options:

  • Visit Google Ads Settings to manage your personalised ad preferences.
  • Visit AboutAds.info to opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies.
  • Use your browser's privacy settings to block third-party cookies entirely.

5. Third-Party Services

Numvexa works with the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics – Traffic analysis and user behaviour insights.
  • Google AdSense – Display advertising served on the website.
  • Google Fonts – Typography loaded from Google's servers (may log your IP address).
  • Unsplash – Some images on the blog are served from Unsplash's CDN.

We are not responsible for the data practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the limited information processed on our website. Since calculator inputs never leave your browser, the primary security risk on Numvexa is the contact form — and we use standard encryption (HTTPS) for all data in transit.

That said, no method of internet transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee that information sent to us will never be intercepted. We will notify you if we become aware of any breach that affects your data.

7. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding personal data we hold about you:

  • Right to access – Request a copy of any personal data we have about you.
  • Right to correction – Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to deletion – Request that we delete your personal information.
  • Right to opt out – Opt out of personalised advertising via Google's settings (see Section 4).

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at rajamahakul90@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Children's Privacy

Numvexa is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. Our financial calculators are intended for adults making informed financial decisions. If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us through the contact form, please notify us immediately and we will delete that information promptly.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our practices change or as required by law. When we do, the revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of Numvexa after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
